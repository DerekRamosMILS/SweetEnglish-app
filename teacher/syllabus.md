# English Learning Syllabus — A1 to C1
**Goal:** Functional English for living in an English-speaking country
**Schedule:** ~5 classes/week · 1 hour per class
**Estimated duration:** 18 months at consistent pace

---

## How to use this syllabus

Each unit has ~4 lessons. Each lesson = 1 hour class.
When you want to generate a lesson, tell Claude:

> "Generate [UNIT_ID]-[LESSON_ID] — e.g. U01-L2"

Claude will produce a full `curriculum.json` for that lesson, ready to upload.

**Lesson structure (every class):**
- ~10 min · Presentation (new vocabulary + phonetics)
- ~15 min · Verbal Drills (guided practice with teacher)
- ~15 min · Dialogue (real conversation applied)
- ~10 min · Written Practice (solo Duolingo-style exercises)
- ~5 min  · Checkpoint (final evaluation)
- ~5 min  · Homework briefing (explain what to study alone)

---

## LEVEL A1 — Survival English
*Target: Can introduce herself, understand basic questions, handle simple daily interactions.*
*Duration: ~10 weeks*

**Grammar:** Verb "to be" (I am, you are) · Subject pronouns · Basic word order
**Vocabulary:** 30 core greeting/intro words

- **U01-L2** · My Name Is... *(name, age, where are you from)*
